How to Split a Large PDF into Separate Files

Last updated: August 2026

A scanned contract with three signatories, a 200-page annual report you only need one chapter from, a merged statement that now needs to go back to individual months for an accountant — splitting a PDF comes up almost as often as combining one. This guide walks through the common ways people need to split a file and how to do each one cleanly.

The Three Kinds of "Splitting"

People asking "how do I split a PDF" usually mean one of three different things. Picking the right one saves a step:

  • Extract a page range. You want pages 4–9 out of a 40-page document as their own file — say, just your section of a shared report.
  • Break into equal chunks. A 300-page scanned book needs to become ten 30-page files because an upload portal or email limit won't take the whole thing at once.
  • One file per page. Each page needs to become its own separate PDF — common when a multi-invoice scan needs to be filed as individual invoices.

  2. Choose your split method: a specific page range, equal-sized chunks, or one PDF per page, depending on which of the three cases above matches your situation.
  3. Review the preview to confirm the page boundaries land where you expect, especially if the source document doesn't have obvious chapter breaks.
  4. Download the result — depending on the method chosen, this may be a single extracted file or a .zip containing multiple PDFs.

Finding the Right Page Numbers First

Before splitting, it helps to know exactly which pages you need. If the PDF has printed page numbers that don't match the actual PDF page count (common in scanned books with a cover, blank pages, or a title page before page "1"), open the file in any PDF viewer and note the PDF page index shown in the viewer's toolbar, not the number printed on the page itself. This avoids the classic off-by-one or off-by-three mistake.

Splitting vs. Deleting Pages

If your actual goal is "remove pages 2 and 5, keep everything else as one file," that's not really splitting — it's page deletion. Our Delete Pages tool is the more direct route: select the pages to remove and download the trimmed file, rather than splitting into pieces and re-merging the parts you want to keep.
