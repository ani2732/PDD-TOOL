About This Tool

This tool rearranges the pages inside a single PDF. Every page appears as a thumbnail in its current order; move pages left or right with the arrows, remove strays with , and each thumbnail's label shows both its new position and where it came from ("Page 3 — was 7"), so the change is always visible. The saved file matches the preview exactly. Everything runs in your browser — no uploads.

When You Need This

  • Pages scanned out of sequence: a stack fed into the scanner in the wrong order, or back sides interleaved incorrectly from two-sided originals.
  • Restructuring a filing bundle: moving an annexure ahead of another, or shifting the covering letter to the front — see our filing preparation checklist for the order reviewers expect.
  • Quick cleanup: reorder and drop stray pages in one pass instead of splitting and re-merging.

To rearrange whole files rather than pages within one file, use the Merge tool, which previews all files' pages in merge order. To fix page orientation, use Rotate first.

Why Page Order Goes Wrong — and How This Fixes It

Page order breaks most often at the scanner, not at the desk. A duplex (two-sided) scan run through a single-sided feeder can interleave fronts and backs in the wrong sequence; a jammed feeder can drop a sheet back in at the wrong point after a re-scan; and combining PDFs exported from different applications can leave a table of contents or cover sheet buried in the middle instead of at the front. Rather than rescanning, this tool lets you fix the sequence directly on the file you already have.

How It Works Under the Hood

Every thumbnail you see is rendered client-side with pdf.js directly from your file — nothing is uploaded to generate the previews. When you save, pdf-lib copies each page's underlying content into a new document in your chosen order; it's a page re-sequence, not a re-render, so text stays selectable and image quality is untouched.

A Practical Workflow

  1. Load the PDF and scan the thumbnails top-to-bottom — misordered pages are usually obvious at a glance (a signature page before the content it belongs to, or a table before its heading).
  2. Use the arrows to move pages into position one at a time; the "was N" label under each thumbnail tracks where it originally came from, so you can always sanity-check the reshuffle against the original.
  3. Drop any stray pages with the remove button in the same pass, rather than doing a separate delete step afterward.
  4. Save — the download matches the thumbnail order exactly, so what you see is what you get.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I undo a reorder after saving?
The tool doesn't keep version history — if you want to compare against the original, keep the source file until you've confirmed the new order is correct.
Does reordering affect image or text quality?
No. Pages are copied, not redrawn, so the saved file's content matches the original pages exactly — only their position changes.
Can I reorder pages from more than one PDF into a single file?
This tool reorders pages within one file. To combine and order pages from multiple files, use the Merge tool, which lets you set file order before merging.
